What we see · Astra and Darby, our perspective

You keep making belonging tangible

Our reading: you keep making belonging tangible. A player can see someone like themselves on the field. A student can walk through a studio door. A friend can take a seat at a table, or leave a landmark in a world you build together.

And not everything has to mean something. A good catch, a ridiculous flight, a song that arrives at the right moment. This world should give you those too.

Across these chapters, enjoyment often becomes an invitation. That is the connection the growing crew makes visible. The repeating shape is a threshold: a studio door, a place on a team, a classroom, a portal. You make those thresholds easier to cross. The game should do the same for a friend arriving here for the first time.

How the game carries that

  • Passengers climb aboard with every stamp. Belonging, made into a mechanic.
  • Landings are graded but never failed. Arriving matters more than the score.
  • Dodgeball and Joy Circuit are play for its own sake: the ridiculous flight, the good catch, a friend on the same device.
  • Keepsakes reward curiosity with your own words and dates.
  • Relaxed flight and Just read it. Your life is never locked behind a win.
