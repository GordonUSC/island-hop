# Island Hop · October 2026

The October entry is an original Canvas 2D archipelago. The existing Three.js balloon game, flight courses, Joy Circuit, saved creations and settings remain at `flight.html`. Old browser storage keys are untouched. October stores its keepsakes, best scores, creations and preferences independently in `islandhop-october-2026-v1`.

## Play

Eight open islands: direct a three-shot scene, change a character's craft, connect guests with rotating bridges, play a three-lane rhythm phrase, dodge/catch/return a rally, lay a limited lantern path, stack seven arcade layers, and solve a nine-lantern switch puzzle. The Make / Connect / Play voyage ends with a balloon carrying the colors of the keepsakes you earned.

Every island supports keyboard and touch. Timed games wait for Start. Pause stops the game clock; leaving the tab pauses play. Sound is opt-in. Gentle pace widens timing windows and slows action. Calm motion follows the OS on first visit and removes decorative motion. Bests and keepsakes persist locally. There are no accounts, trackers, network calls or external dependencies in the October layer.

## Art and content

The new scenes, island silhouettes, characters, icons and synthesized tones were authored specifically for this game in Canvas/Web Audio. The archived flight retains its existing models, Higgsfield skies, audio and original credits; its environmental `cloud_layers` lighting is credited in `game.js` to Greg Zaal / Poly Haven, CC0. This update does not relicense existing assets.

The eight islands are playful interpretations of current themes: making with AI, teaching game craft, GGP opportunity, music, dodgeball, Gordtopia, alumni creativity and desert celebration. Travelers are fictional. No private source messages, schedules, applicant information or research excerpts are included. The original flight's story copy was shortened to remove itinerary detail while keeping its activities and existing public Gordtopia image.

## Design decision

Audience: Gordon and friends, phone or keyboard. Goal: produce a visible change in the first minute. Selected direction: a handcrafted overhead archipelago, using shallow extruded forms, a readable game board, warm landscape colors and eight different verbs. Alternative: another cinematic 3D tour. The original spatial balloon experience remains accessible, while the October playspaces prioritize readable patterns and precise input.

Experience goals: direct a scene with intention; see timing change a character; solve a connection; catch a groove; read and return a rally; find an efficient scenic crossing; feel an alignment; discover a toggle pattern. Achiever hooks: stars and local bests. Explorer hooks: open islands and alternate route layouts. Social hook: share the same device and compare local performances, without claiming online multiplayer. Competitive hook: improve personal timing/efficiency. Lore never requires a win.

First review found a timing problem: a phrase starting while a newcomer read instructions. The revised games wait for an explicit Start. A completed voyage now pays off with the keepsake balloon. Screenshots and real-input QA are retained in the build workspace.
